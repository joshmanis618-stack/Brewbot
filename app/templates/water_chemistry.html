Water Chemistry Calculator

Enter your source water profile and mineral additions to see the resulting water chemistry. Mineral doses are entered in grams per batch.

Residual Alkalinity (RA)

RA = Alkalinity (as CaCO₃) − Ca/1.4 − Mg/1.7. Negative RA lowers mash pH; positive RA raises it. Target RA −50 to +50 for pale beers, −50 to +100 for darker beers.

Mash pH

Estimated using the simplified Kolbach model. Accuracy improves when grist color (°Lovibond) is entered. Target mash pH is 5.2–5.6 for most beer styles.

Cl:SO₄ Ratio

Higher SO₄ accentuates hop bitterness; higher Cl rounds out malt sweetness. Adjust to match your style target.
