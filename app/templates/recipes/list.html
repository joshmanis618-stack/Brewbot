Creating a recipe

Click New Recipe to open the recipe builder. Give your recipe a name, choose a beer type and style, then add fermentables, hops, yeast, and any miscellaneous ingredients.

Recipe card stats
  • OG — original gravity. A typical pale ale is around 1.050; a strong stout might be 1.080+.
  • ABV — estimated alcohol by volume after fermentation.
  • IBU — bitterness level. Under 20 is mild; over 60 is very bitter.
  • Vol — batch size in liters.

The colored dot on each card shows the approximate beer color based on SRM.

Scale button

The Scale button (arrows icon) opens the recipe scaler, where you can adjust the batch size and system efficiency. All ingredient amounts are recalculated proportionally and saved as a new recipe — the original is not changed.
