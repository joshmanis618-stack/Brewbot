Proofing / Dilution

Based on the Pearce equation with a contraction correction. Alcohol-water mixtures contract on mixing (up to ~3.5% at 50% ABV). Always verify the final proof with a certified alcoholmeter at 60°F / 15.6°C for TTB/HMRC compliance.

Hydrometer Correction

Spirit hydrometers read true proof at 60°F. Above 60°F the spirit expands, making it appear weaker; below 60°F it appears stronger. Apply the correction before recording a proof statement.

Wash Yield

Compares the alcohol in the wash to the theoretical maximum spirits volume at a target cut strength. Still efficiency accounts for losses to foreshots, still holdup, and unrecovered tails.

TTB Age Statement Note

A "Straight" designation (e.g. Straight Bourbon Whiskey) requires a minimum of 2 years aging in new charred oak. The age statement must reflect calendar time from fill date to bottling date. Use the Barrel Registry age tracker to monitor fill date and proof.
