TOSNA Protocol

TOSNA (Tontitown Staggered Nutrient Additions) uses organic nitrogen (Fermaid-O) only, split into four equal additions at 24 h, 48 h, 72 h, and 1/3 sugar depletion. No DAP is used, which reduces harsh off-flavours from ammonia.

TOSNA 2.0

Adds a small amount of DAP (50% of YAN) alongside Fermaid-O to reduce the total grams used and lower cost. Suitable for experienced meadmakers.

SNA Classic

Uses Fermaid-K (a blended nutrient with vitamins, minerals, and both organic and inorganic N) together with DAP. More traditional approach, slightly less clean than pure TOSNA.

1/3 Sugar Depletion

The 4th addition happens when roughly 1/3 of the original sugars have been consumed — calculated as OG minus (OG − 1.000)/3. At this point yeast are dividing rapidly and nutrient demand is highest. Measure with your hydrometer or refractometer and add the 4th dose when you reach this gravity.

YAN target formula

Target YAN (ppm) = OGpts × 2.5 × yeast factor. Honey contributes 5–10 ppm YAN naturally, so most of the nitrogen must come from additions. High-gravity meads (> 1.120) are especially nutrient-hungry.
